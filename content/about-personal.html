The person in between

A little more me.

Learning goes both ways.

From helping someone find their words in the Writing Center to working through a math problem or a piece of music, I’ve spent a lot of time learning how to listen. I care about making room for questions and helping people feel comfortable asking them.

The everyday work matters, too.

Helping at China Garden in Baxter has meant serving customers, keeping records, making menus, and lending a hand wherever needed. Alongside my work in libraries, it’s a reminder that care and attention show up in small, practical ways.
