Hi, I’m Ammy! I’m a master’s student in Interdisciplinary Data Science at Duke University, a researcher, a teacher, and a musician. I grew up in the Brainerd Lakes Area of Minnesota, and I’ve always had a hard time choosing just one thing to be curious about.

At Carleton College, that meant majoring in mathematics and Chinese and minoring in music performance. At Duke, it means exploring machine learning and data systems while asking questions about the people building and using them.

These days, I’m studying how language-model agents’ words line up with their actions at TRUST Lab and helping students learn data engineering. Before that, I spent a summer working on insurance claims models at Liberty Mutual. Across those experiences, I keep coming back to the same things: asking careful questions, making ideas understandable, and learning from other people.

Outside that work, there’s usually music, a book, or a journal nearby. I like slow bike rides, thoughtful gifts, dim sum, and trying something I don’t quite know how to do yet.
