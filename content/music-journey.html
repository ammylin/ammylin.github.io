How it started

“Band or orchestra?”
“Both!”

I started learning the flute and viola when I was a sixth grader at Forestview Middle School. Before that, I played the piano and wanted to try something new. With the guidance, wisdom, and support of those involved in Brainerd’s music program, I continued playing the flute and viola in the band and orchestra programs, respectively, for the rest of my time in the district.

I continued in college, minoring in music performance. There, I joined the Carleton Chinese Music Ensemble and picked up the dizi (Chinese bamboo flute). My successes on the dizi are in no part disconnected from the strong musical education and foundation that I received from the wonderful mentors that I’ve been able to work with.

  1. Growing up

    Band and orchestra

    Through Brainerd’s music programs, I played in state honor bands and the MMEA All-State Orchestra. I also played gigs around town, usually with my string quartet.

  2. At Carleton

    Learning the dizi

    I joined the Chinese Music Ensemble and studied with Gao Hong. I’ve had opportunities to play at the Schubert Club, Minnesota Orchestra pre-concert series, and Mayo Clinic, among others.

  3. 2024

    The YourClassical Prize

    I won the inaugural Karin Larson YourClassical Prize with an entry celebrating women dizi performers and my teacher Gao Hong. I didn’t expect to be a finalist when I submitted my recording and essays. In fact, I wasn’t sure if I was going to submit anything at all!
