How it started

“Band or orchestra?”
“Both!”

That was my answer in sixth grade. I’d already been playing piano, and I wanted to try something new. Flute and viola became part of my life through the Brainerd music programs, with teachers and ensembles that gave me room to grow.

At Carleton, I added the dizi, a Chinese bamboo flute, through the Chinese Music Ensemble and my teacher Gao Hong. It became a way to connect my love of music with my Chinese studies—and an invitation to find a little more confidence in my own voice.

  1. The beginning

    Piano, then two paths

    Afternoon piano lessons led to flute in band and viola in orchestra. Eventually, that meant honor ensembles, the MMEA All-State Orchestra, and string-quartet gigs around town.

  2. At Carleton

    A new instrument

    Alongside a music performance minor, I joined the Chinese Music Ensemble and learned the dizi. Performances took me to places including the Schubert Club and Minnesota Orchestra pre-concert series.

  3. 2024

    A meaningful milestone

    I won the inaugural Karin Larson YourClassical Prize with an entry celebrating women who play the dizi and my teacher Gao Hong. It led to recording at MPR’s Maud Moon Weyerhaeuser Studio.

  4. Sharing the music

    From performing to teaching

    I’ve taught individual and group music lessons and shared the dizi through a YourClassical Class Notes virtual concert for young listeners. Helping someone discover an instrument is a joy of its own.
